The journey, week by week

  1. Visit 1 · 20 min

    Free doctor consultation + skin analysis

    The doctor tells dullness, surface spots and melasma apart. Melasma does not go straight to laser, and IPL can worsen it, so this assessment sets your path.

  2. Visit 1, same day

    Start: Tone Start, Laser Facial + LHALA Peel

    Tone Start, Rp 799,000: Laser Facial then a doctor LHALA Peel. Brighter and smoother from the first session; that is surface renewal, not the final result.

  3. Weeks 2–12

    Programme: 3 or 6 sessions, every 2–4 weeks

    Surface spots move up to Glow Intensive: a doctor brightening peel and laser facial. Week 4: surface spots fade. Week 8: an honest assessment. Deep pigment and melasma are not finished here, and we say so.

  4. Week 12 onward

    Doctor review: Q-switched laser if needed

    Tone Reset: LHALA Peel and 1064 nm QS laser, a maximum of 3 sessions then a doctor review. Sunscreen every 2–3 hours is part of the programme, not advice, because spots return without it.

FAQ

Questions about this programme

Does this change my skin colour?

No, and no clinic should promise that. What changes is evenness, spots and glow.

My skin is medium-dark. Is laser safe?

The QS laser at 1064 nm is safe for skin types III–V; that is why the device was chosen. The doctor still tests a small area first.

Why a facial first?

Peels and laser work deeper on clean, even skin. The order is part of the method, not an upsell.
