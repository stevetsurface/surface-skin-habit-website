A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

It’s okay to
have questions.

Does this change my skin colour?

No, and no clinic should promise that. What changes is evenness, spots and glow.

My skin is medium-dark. Is laser safe?

The QS laser at 1064 nm is safe for skin types III–V; that is why the device was chosen. The doctor still tests a small area first.

Why a facial first?

Peels and laser work deeper on clean, even skin. The order is part of the method, not an upsell.
