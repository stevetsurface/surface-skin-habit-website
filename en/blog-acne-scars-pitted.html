THE SURFACE JOURNAL

Indented acne scars: why a facial isn't enough, and what works

Indented scars are held down by scar tissue underneath. What works is microneedling, subcision and time, in a clear order.

Book a free consultation ↗Explore below ↓

2026-10-20

There are two kinds of acne scars. Dark marks (PIH) are pigment and belong to the Tone pathway: brightening peels and laser. Indented scars are structural: scar tissue pulls the skin down. Facials and light abrasion do not reach them.

The order at Surface. First, active acne must be controlled for 8 weeks; needling inflamed skin makes scars worse. Second, Satin: a LHALA Peel then Nanoporeless microneedling (Mediderma Nanopore) every 4 weeks, 3–4 sessions, with AQ Recovery Serum or salmon PDRN. Third, for deep tethered scars, Scar Release: doctor subcision to free the tether, paired with Hydraderm.

Open prices: Diamond Resurface Rp 750,000, Satin Rp 3,200,000 per session, Scar Release Rp 2,450,000 single or Rp 6,600,000 for 3 sessions.

What we do not promise: pitted scars gone completely. What we can promise: shallower scars and more even skin, judged with 3-angle photos at every visit and a doctor review at week 12.

Who should wait: isotretinoin in the last 6 months, keloid history, active infection. The doctor consultation is free at four Jakarta clinics.
