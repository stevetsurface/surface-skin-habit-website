Many people, many skin journeys. A personal approach that remains at the heart of Surface.

Book a free consultation ↗Explore below ↓

A skin habit, built over time.

Surface began in 2014. Over the years, we have cared for people seeking help with acne, pigmentation, texture, sensitivity, and changes in skin quality. Everyone arrives with a different story.

Our approach starts with the concern. Understand what is happening, choose care with a doctor, and review progress together. A course of care gives each visit a purpose beyond the appointment itself.

Today, you can find Surface at Setiabudi, CITOS, Lippo Mall Puri, and TSM Cibubur. Your care stays personal as your needs evolve.
