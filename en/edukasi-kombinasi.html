Treatment combinations

Almost every Surface offer is a combination: a facial or peel that prepares the skin, then one step that works deeper. The order is not accidental.

All devices & proceduresTreatmentsDevices & brandsCombinationsIntervals & timing

Five rules of combining

Cleanse first, then correct

A facial opens pores and lifts dead cells, so the peel or laser after it works on clean, even skin.

The peel opens the way for the device

A chemical peel thins the outer layer, so laser or IPL light reaches deeper at the same energy.

One doctor step in every combination

Medical peels, lasers, HIFU and injections are doctor-only. Facials and RF may be done by therapists.

Calm and protect at the end

PDT or a calming mask, then high-SPF sunscreen before you leave. This is the step that keeps the result.

Some pairs do not share a visit

Mechanical abrasion before a chemical peel, or a peel followed by injection, increases penetration and risk. The doctor decides whether to split them into two visits.

The order in the chair, and what changes when

The six combination patterns on the current Surface menu. Minutes are estimates; the doctor and therapist adjust to your skin.

Facial + LHALA Peel

⏱ 110 min total · ↻ every 2–4 weeks · Used in: Clear StartGlass Skin Facial

Order in the chair

  1. Cleanse & skin check10 min
  2. Facial (Hydraderm or H2O)50 min
  3. Changeover / prep10 min
  4. LHALA Peel applied by the doctor10 min
  5. Neutralise & calm10 min
  6. Sunscreen & aftercare briefing10 min

What changes, when

  • Week 1–2Skin feels smoother and brighter from surface renewal.
  • Week 4After session 2: fewer blackheads, dullness responds first.
  • Week 8After session 3–4: the skin settles. The point where people most often stop, just when it should continue.
  • Week 12Doctor review: enough here, or move up to a device step.

Facial + Peel + PDT

⏱ 140 min total · ↻ every 2–3 weeks · Used in: Acne Reset

Order in the chair

  1. Cleanse & skin check10 min
  2. Hydraderm Facial50 min
  3. Changeover / prep10 min
  4. Targeted SA Peel by the doctor15 min
  5. Neutralise10 min
  6. PDT light therapy20 min
  7. Sunscreen & aftercare10 min

What changes, when

  • Week 1–2Inflamed lesions settle faster. Skin feels less oily.
  • Week 4After session 2: fewer blackheads, fewer new breakouts.
  • Week 8After session 3–4: the breakout pattern stabilises.
  • Week 12Doctor review. If only marks remain, move to a brightening peel or laser.

Facial + Peel + Laser

⏱ 170 min total · ↻ every 2–4 weeks · Used in: Glow Intensive

Order in the chair

  1. Cleanse & skin check10 min
  2. H2O Facial50 min
  3. Changeover / prep10 min
  4. Ultrabrightening Peel by the doctor15 min
  5. Neutralise10 min
  6. Laser Facial, one pass, by the doctor20 min
  7. Calm, sunscreen & aftercare15 min

What changes, when

  • Week 1–2Skin looks fresher and more hydrated.
  • Week 4After session 2: surface spots start to fade.
  • Week 8After session 3–4: honest assessment. Deep pigment and melasma are not finished here.
  • Week 12Doctor review: enough, or move up to the Q-switched laser.

LHALA Peel + Q-switched laser

⏱ 85 min total · ↻ every 3 weeks · Used in: Tone Reset

Order in the chair

  1. Cleanse & 3-angle photos10 min
  2. LHALA Peel by the doctor, shortened contact time10 min
  3. Neutralise & pause10 min
  4. QS Brighten & Toning by the doctor35 min
  5. Calm, sunscreen & aftercare20 min

What changes, when

  • Week 1–2Mild redness for a few hours. Spots may look slightly darker before they fade.
  • Week 4After session 2: tone starts to even out.
  • Week 8After session 3: the doctor MUST reassess before continuing. The Q-switched laser is not sold as 6 consecutive sessions.
  • Week 12Maintenance with peels and strict sunscreen, or a further series on the doctor's assessment.

RF + LHALA Peel

⏱ 85 min total · ↻ every 2–4 weeks · Used in: Peel & LiftContour Signature

Order in the chair

  1. Cleanse & 3-angle photos10 min
  2. Tighten & Contour RF (face and/or neck)30 min
  3. Changeover / prep5 min
  4. LHALA Peel by the doctor10 min
  5. Neutralise10 min
  6. Calm, sunscreen & aftercare10 min

What changes, when

  • Week 1–2Feels temporarily firmer; partly the immediate RF effect, not new collagen.
  • Week 4After session 2: not much visible in photos yet. Normal, and worth knowing before you start.
  • Week 8After session 3–4: collagen building becomes visible. First honest assessment.
  • Week 12Doctor review: continue as maintenance, or move up to HIFU.

HIFU + Meso

⏱ 130 min total · ↻ set by the doctor · Used in: Jawline Define

Order in the chair

  1. Cleanse, 3-angle photos & area marking15 min
  2. Topical numbing cream20 min
  3. HIFU double chin by the doctor40 min
  4. Pause & calm10 min
  5. Meso Lipo face, injected by the doctor25 min
  6. Calm & aftercare20 min

What changes, when

  • Week 1–2Mild swelling or tenderness for a few days. Injection points for 1–2 days.
  • Week 4Skin feels denser; shape has not changed yet.
  • Week 8–12The HIFU result shows fully as collagen remodels.
  • Month 6–12HIFU is repeated only on the doctor's assessment.
