Intervals, programmes & waiting times

The interval is not a suggestion; it is part of the treatment. Skin needs time to respond before the next step, and too long a gap lets the result slip back.

Programme length & validity

  • Single1 visit, no validity period.
  • 3-session packValid 3 months from payment. At 2–4 week intervals it finishes in 6–12 weeks, leaving only 1–7 weeks of slack. One reschedule can use it up.
  • 6-session packValid 9 months from payment. A 4–6 month relationship with your skin; intervals set with the doctor.
  • ExtensionOnce, up to 8 weeks, with Clinic Manager approval.
  • If validity lapsesSessions taken are recharged at the single price; the balance comes back as credit or refund. You lose the package benefit, not your money.

When your next visit should be

Your next appointment is booked before you leave, not chased by message afterwards.

  • Facials & hydrationNext visit within 4 weeks.
  • Acne (SA peel, Acne Shots)Within 3 weeks.
  • Skin tone (laser, peels)Within 6 weeks.
  • Tightening (RF, HIFU)Within 8 weeks, then on the doctor's schedule.

When to wait

  • Isotretinoin (oral acne medication)Postpone medium peels, laser and microneedling until 6 months after stopping.
  • Heavy sun exposureAvoid 2 weeks before and 6 weeks after laser or peels. Sunscreen every 2–3 hours outdoors.
  • Treatment at another clinicLeave 4 weeks and tell the doctor what was done.
  • Pregnant or breastfeedingFacials and PDT are fine. Strong peels, laser, RF, HIFU and injections wait; the doctor sets a special protocol.
  • Topical retinoids, scrubs, waxingStop 3–5 days before a peel or laser, and 3–5 days after.

This is not a diagnosis

The information here is general. It is not a diagnosis, nor medical advice for your own skin. Only a doctor who examines you in person can decide what is appropriate. A doctor consultation at Surface is always free.
