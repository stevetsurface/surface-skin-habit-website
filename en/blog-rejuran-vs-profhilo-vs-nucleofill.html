Rejuran, Profhilo, Nucleofill: the difference, and which one is for you

The three skin boosters we are asked about most on WhatsApp. All three are injected by a doctor, but their goals differ.

Book a free consultation ↗Explore below ↓

2026-10-20

A skin booster is not a filler. A filler adds volume at one point; a booster is injected thinly and evenly to improve skin quality from within. That is why it is the Enhance stage at Surface, after tone is even and texture is smooth.

Rejuran (salmon-DNA PDRN, PharmaResearch Korea) is for regeneration: skin quality, fine lines, acne marks. Healer, HB Plus and Pro variants. Profhilo (stabilised hyaluronic acid, IBSA Italy) is for bio-remodelling: deep hydration and firmness, the 'bouncy' effect. Nucleofill (polynucleotides, Promoitalia) sits in between: hydration plus collagen stimulation.

How the doctor chooses: acne marks and regeneration, Rejuran; loose, dry skin, Profhilo; a bit of both, Nucleofill, sometimes combined with Profhilo. Usually 2–3 sessions, then yearly maintenance.

At Surface, Reserve Duo (LHALA Peel + Rejuran) is Rp 8,000,000; other booster prices are given by the doctor at the consultation, because agent and dose are tailored. Every brand we inject is named.

What the doctor needs to know before injecting: clotting disorders or blood thinners, pregnancy and breastfeeding, active infection, and keloid history. The consultation is free.
