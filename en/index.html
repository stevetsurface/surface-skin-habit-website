LET’S START WITH YOU

What’s on your skin’s mind?

Six pathways. Your doctor helps you find a personal plan.

01 / CLEAR

Acne & congestion

Acne is the number-one concern at Surface. We start with a deep cleanse, then the doctor adds a peel or procedure to match your skin.

↗02 / TONE

Dullness, spots & uneven tone

Dullness and dark spots are the second most common concern. An H2O facial opens the way, then a doctor peel and laser even out your tone.

↗03 / TEXTURE

Acne scars & pores

Texture is the stage after tone is even: large pores, roughness and indented acne scars. Facials do not reach them; resurfacing, microneedling and subcision by the doctor do.

↗04 / CALM

Sensitive, redness & barrier

Skin that reddens and stings is a different customer: often not yet able to take microneedling or strong peels. Calm soothes and strengthens the barrier first, and is the natural entry before any other pathway.

↗05 / TIGHTEN

Sagging, lines & contour

For skin that is starting to loosen, or contours you want sharper. From no-downtime radiofrequency up to doctor HIFU.

↗06 / ENHANCE

Skin boosters & Botox

The door for people who already know they want an injectable. Two shelves: skin boosters for skin quality, and botulinum toxin for dynamic lines. Only the doctor chooses agent and dose, and the price is given before you decide.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

It’s okay to
have questions.

Is the doctor consultation really free?

Yes. Every customer starts with a free doctor consultation, including on the first visit. The doctor recommends the treatment that suits your skin.

Are website prices the same as in the clinic?

Yes. Single, 3-session and 6-session prices apply at every Surface clinic. There is no extra discount on top of package prices.

Can I use remaining package sessions at another clinic?

Yes. Your treatment history and remaining sessions are valid at all four Surface clinics.

How long is a package valid?

A 3-session package is valid for 3 months and a 6-session package for 9 months from the payment date. Your next visit is scheduled before you leave.

What if I'm not sure which treatment fits?

You don't need to choose. Pick your concern, then come in for a free doctor consultation. The doctor recommends the treatment.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp at 0812 2234 6115. Tell us your concern and your nearest clinic and we'll find you a free doctor consultation slot.
