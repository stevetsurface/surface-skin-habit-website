Pollogen RF

A radiofrequency current heats the deeper layer of skin evenly until collagen fibres tighten and new collagen production is triggered.

In more depth

An RF current meets resistance as it passes through tissue, and that resistance produces heat. The heat spreads evenly through the dermis rather than concentrating at one point as HIFU does, so it feels more like a warm massage and leaves almost no downtime.

Because it works more superficially, the effect per session is gentler and shows best when run as a course. RF is not absorbed by melanin, so it is safe for all skin types, and it is often used on areas too thin for other devices, such as around the eyes.

What it is used for

  • Mild to moderate laxity
  • Fine lines
  • Facial and body contour
  • Loss of bounce around the eyes
  • Cellulite

Wait if

  • You are pregnant
  • You have a pacemaker or other electronic implant
  • You have a metal implant in the treatment area
  • There is an active skin infection

Afterwards

  • Drink enough water afterwards
  • Moisturise as usual, there are no special restrictions
  • Sunscreen every day
  • The effect shows best after a course of several sessions
