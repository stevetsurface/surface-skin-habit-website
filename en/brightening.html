BEHIND YOUR PLAN

What does it actually do?

HOW IT WORKS

Biaxis Q-Switched Nd:YAG

Very short laser pulses break clusters of pigment inside the skin into fine particles, which the body then clears on its own.

See the skin explainer ↗HOW IT WORKS

IPL Cellec

Broad-spectrum light is absorbed by pigment and fine vessels, then converted to heat that makes both fade.

See the skin explainer ↗HOW IT WORKS

Chemical Peel

An acid solution loosens the bonds between dead skin cells so the top layer sheds and is replaced by newer skin.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

It’s okay to
have questions.

Does this change my skin colour?

No, and no clinic should promise that. What changes is evenness, spots and glow.

My skin is medium-dark. Is laser safe?

The QS laser at 1064 nm is safe for skin types III–V; that is why the device was chosen. The doctor still tests a small area first.

Why a facial first?

Peels and laser work deeper on clean, even skin. The order is part of the method, not an upsell.
