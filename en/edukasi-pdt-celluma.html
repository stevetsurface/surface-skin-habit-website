TREATMENT GUIDE

PDT Celluma

An LED panel emits light at specific wavelengths that skin cells absorb, calming inflammation and speeding recovery.

How it works

Blue light is absorbed by the bacteria involved in acne and disrupts their ability to survive. Red light travels deeper and acts on the cells' mitochondria, which makes the repair process run faster.

There is no damaging heat and no peeling, so LED is often used to close a session after another procedure, or as an option for skin too sensitive for other devices.

What it addresses

  • Actively inflamed acne
  • Redness after a procedure
  • Skin recovery
  • Sensitive skin that cannot tolerate other procedures

Discuss with your doctor

  • You are taking medication that makes skin highly light-sensitive
  • You have a history of light-triggered seizures
  • There is a skin lesion a doctor has not yet examined

Aftercare

  • No restrictions; you can put make-up straight back on
  • Sunscreen as usual

The information here is general. It is not a diagnosis, nor medical advice for your own skin. Only a doctor who examines you in person can decide what is appropriate. A doctor consultation at Surface is always free.

WHAT HAPPENS IN YOUR SKIN

Light, without abrasion

LED treatment uses selected wavelengths. Blue light targets acne-associated bacteria; red light is used for different skin responses. It does not scrub or peel the skin.

A LITTLE PREPARATION HELPS

Recovery, without the guesswork.

Most expected reactions are temporary. Knowing what is usual—and what needs a call—helps you plan with confidence.

TIME TO PLAN

Downtime

Usually little or no social downtime from LED alone. Recovery may differ when it follows extraction, peeling, or needling.

OFTEN TEMPORARY

What you may notice

Mild temporary warmth, redness, or irritation can occur.

Potential risks and side effects

Light-sensitive conditions or medicines can make a reaction more likely. Eye protection and device-specific instructions remain important.

Before your treatment

Mention light sensitivity and all medicines. Ask whether the session is LED alone or part of a combined procedure.

When to contact us

Contact the clinic if discomfort is increasing, redness is spreading, blisters appear, or recovery is different from what was explained. Seek urgent medical care for vision changes, breathing or swallowing difficulty, or severe symptoms.
