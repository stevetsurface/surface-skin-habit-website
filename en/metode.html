The Skin Habit Cycle™

Every Surface customer moves through the same cycle, whichever pathway they are on. The doctor assesses on every turn; you only need to show up on schedule.

How Surface works

Skin Habit™ · Three things we combine

A doctor at every step, a plan with dates, and a monthly habit. Other clinics have one or two; Skin Habit is all three at once.

Learn the Skin Habit Cycle
  1. Visit 1 · 20 min

    Begin · Free doctor consultation + skin analysis

    The doctor looks at your skin, screens for anything that changes the protocol, and picks the pathway: Clear, Tone, Texture, Calm, Tighten or Enhance.

  2. Visit 1 · same day

    Start · One visit, one price: Rp 799,000

    A therapist facial closed with one doctor step. You leave with a written plan: the next visit date and the review date.

  3. Weeks 2–12 · every 2–4 weeks

    Correct · A 3- or 6-session programme

    Facial plus a device or medical peel by the doctor, sold as a programme. Every visit has a date; we do the reminding.

  4. Week 12 · photos repeated

    Review · The doctor assesses at week 12

    Photos and analysis repeated with the same light and angle. The doctor decides: continue, step up to Refine, or move to Maintain.

  5. Monthly · Skin Habit Plan

    Maintain · One Start visit every month

    Results kept by habit, not by a new package. Every 12 weeks, back to Review.

Refine

The most targeted device or injectable, only on the doctor's decision at Review.
