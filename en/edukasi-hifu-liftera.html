TREATMENT GUIDE

HIFU Liftera

Ultrasound waves are focused to a single point deep in the skin, heating it until the connective tissue contracts and the body lays down new collagen.

Session

70 minutes

Interval

every 2–4 weeks

What it feels like

Brief heat, with some soreness over bony areas.

Recovery

Almost none. Mild redness and tenderness can last a few hours.

How it works

Ultrasound energy passes through the surface without heating it, then converges at one focal point at a set depth, much like a magnifying glass concentrating sunlight. At that point the temperature rises enough to make the connective tissue contract on the spot.

The initial tightening comes from that contraction, and it continues over 8–12 weeks as the body builds new collagen in the heated area. Because ultrasound does not depend on skin colour, it is safe across all skin types.

What it addresses

  • Skin that has begun to loosen
  • Sagging cheeks and a softening jawline
  • Fullness under the chin
  • Lines around the eyes
  • Drooping eyelids

Discuss with your doctor

  • You are pregnant
  • There is an infection or open wound in the treatment area
  • You have an implant or recent filler in that area
  • You have a bleeding disorder

Aftercare

  • Drink enough water and avoid alcohol for 24 hours
  • Results build gradually over 8–12 weeks, so do not judge them in the first week
  • Sunscreen every day
  • Hold off on firm facial massage for a week

The information here is general. It is not a diagnosis, nor medical advice for your own skin. Only a doctor who examines you in person can decide what is appropriate. A doctor consultation at Surface is always free.
