Programme

The journey, week by week

Design placeholder

SHOOT: red PDT light over the face, customer relaxed. 1200×800, 3:2.

  1. Visit 1 · 20 min

    Free doctor consultation + skin analysis

    The doctor tells sensitive skin, a damaged barrier and rosacea apart. What you currently use is assessed too; the irritation often comes from the routine, not your skin.

  2. Visit 1, same day

    Start: Calm Start, Oxy Facial sensitive protocol

    The gentlest hydro-exfoliation on our menu, no aggressive extraction. No peel on the first visit for skin that is reactive.

  3. Weeks 2–12

    Strengthen: H2O Sensi + PDT, every 2–3 weeks

    Red light calms and aids recovery. Week 4: reactive redness eases. Week 8: the barrier is steadier; the doctor decides whether a short-contact peel is allowed yet.

  4. Week 12 onward

    Protect: routine and maintenance

    Sunscreen and a minimal routine set by the doctor. Monthly maintenance on the Skin Habit Plan uses H2O Sensi as the monthly facial.

What we promise

  • No peel or device on the first visit if the skin is reactive

What we don't promise

FAQ

Questions about this programme

Is a facial safe for sensitive skin?

H2O Sensi is built for it: no Sebo solution, no aggressive extraction, closed with a calming mask. The doctor assesses first if the skin is reactive.

Can I have a peel?

Not on the first visit. Once the barrier is steady, the doctor can use a short-contact peel.

What does it cost?

H2O Sensi is Rp 1,200,000, with PDT Rp 1,450,000. The barrier protocol is set by the doctor and priced before you decide.
