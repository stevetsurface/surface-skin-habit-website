WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

Care, with clear boundaries.

Our commitment

  • No peel or device on the first visit if the skin is reactive
  • The doctor assesses the barrier before anything touches the skin
  • Your home products are assessed, not just added to
  • Stop any time the skin reacts; sessions are not forfeited

Before you decide

  • No promise that skin will 'stop being sensitive'; sensitivity is managed, not erased.
  • No IPL or laser for redness without a doctor assessment.
  • No discounts to try a harsher treatment.

Surface & pigment

Epidermis

Your outer barrier. Surface texture and some pigment concerns involve this area. Pigment can also lie deeper, so a dark spot is not a diagnosis.

Gentle cleansing, selected superficial peels, and doctor-selected pigment treatments work in different ways.

Explore related care ↗
Collagen & texture

Dermis

A supportive layer containing collagen, elastin, vessels, and skin structures. Fine lines and scars are more complex than surface roughness.

Microneedling and RF can trigger different renewal responses; they are not interchangeable and do not use the same depth.

Explore related care ↗
Volume & cushioning

Subcutaneous tissue

Fat and connective tissue beneath the dermis help shape and cushion an area. Less volume is not automatically a better outcome.

Contouring needs an assessment of anatomy and the actual device or injectable product—not just a treatment name.

Explore related care ↗
Support & lifting

Deeper connective support

Deeper connective structures, including the facial SMAS, are beneath the skin. Anatomy varies by area; this is not a separate universal skin layer.

Focused ultrasound uses selected focal depths. Your doctor chooses the device, area, and settings; the illustration does not prescribe a depth.

Explore related care ↗
Expression & movement

Facial muscle

Expression muscles sit beneath and interact with the skin and connective tissues. They are distinct from the dermis.

Botulinum toxin reduces activity in selected muscles to soften expression lines. It does not exfoliate or tighten a skin layer.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

It’s okay to
have questions.

Is a facial safe for sensitive skin?

H2O Sensi is built for it: no Sebo solution, no aggressive extraction, closed with a calming mask. The doctor assesses first if the skin is reactive.

Can I have a peel?

Not on the first visit. Once the barrier is steady, the doctor can use a short-contact peel.

What does it cost?

H2O Sensi is Rp 1,200,000, with PDT Rp 1,450,000. The barrier protocol is set by the doctor and priced before you decide.
