Sunspots, freckles & uneven tone

  1. 01START HERE

    Identify the pigment

    Sunspots, freckles, post-acne marks, and melasma need different plans. A new or changing spot needs medical assessment first.

  2. 02YOUR PLAN

    Protect, then select

    Start with sun protection and doctor-advised skincare. Discuss a pigment procedure only if suitable; melasma needs particular care.

    Review pigment & recovery

    Check response and irritation before deciding whether to continue, change, or pause.

TEXTURE

Acne scars & texture

  1. 01START HERE

    Separate scars from active acne

    Control active acne first. Your doctor assesses scar shape, depth, and skin tone.

  2. 02YOUR PLAN

    Choose a scar-specific approach

    Microneedling may be one option. Different scars may need a different procedure or referral; a facial is not equivalent to scar treatment.

  3. 03REVIEW

    Allow healing, then compare

    Review healing and gradual change using comparable photographs. Further sessions depend on response.

CALM

Sensitivity & redness

  1. 01START HERE

    Find the cause

    Redness can have several causes. Tell the doctor about stinging, flaking, products, and recent treatments.

  2. 02YOUR PLAN

    Simplify & support

    Follow a gentle, clinician-advised routine. A procedure can wait if skin is irritated; doing less may be the right next step.

  3. 03REVIEW

    Reassess comfort first

    Discuss optional supportive care only when suitable. Persistent or worsening symptoms need reassessment.

TIGHTEN

Firmness & contour

  1. 01START HERE

    Identify what is changing

    Skin laxity, volume changes, and expression lines are different concerns. Your doctor checks anatomy and expectations.

  2. 02YOUR PLAN

    Choose one suitable direction

    Discuss RF or focused ultrasound if appropriate. They are alternatives with different targets—not automatic steps in one session.

    Recover & allow time

    Follow aftercare and review at the doctor-set time before considering another procedure.

ENHANCE

Skin quality & expression lines

  1. 01START HERE

    Name the actual goal

    Hydration, expression lines, and contour are different goals. Discuss which one matters to you.

  2. 02YOUR PLAN

    Match the product to the goal

    Botulinum toxin acts on selected muscles; skin boosters vary by product. They are not interchangeable. Discuss suitability and risks first.

  3. 03REVIEW

    Review before adding more

    Let the response develop and attend the agreed review. More treatment is not always the next step.
