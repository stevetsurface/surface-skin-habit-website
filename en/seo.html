LET’S START WITH YOU

What’s on your skin’s mind?

Six pathways. Your doctor helps you find a personal plan.

01 / CLEAR

Acne & congestion

Acne is the number-one concern at Surface. We start with a deep cleanse, then the doctor adds a peel or procedure to match your skin.

↗02 / TONE

Sunspots, freckles & uneven tone

Sunspots, freckles, melasma and dark acne marks can look similar, but need different care. Start with a doctor assessment to understand your pigmentation and choose an appropriate plan.

↗03 / TEXTURE

Acne scars & pores

Texture is the stage after tone is even: large pores, roughness and indented acne scars. Facials do not reach them; resurfacing, microneedling and subcision by the doctor do.

↗04 / CALM

Sensitive, redness & barrier

Skin that reddens and stings is a different customer: often not yet able to take microneedling or strong peels. Calm soothes and strengthens the barrier first, and is the natural entry before any other pathway.

↗05 / TIGHTEN

Sagging, lines & contour

For skin that is starting to loosen, or contours you want sharper. From no-downtime radiofrequency up to doctor HIFU.

↗06 / ENHANCE

Skin boosters & Botox

The door for people who already know they want an injectable. Two shelves: skin boosters for skin quality, and botulinum toxin for dynamic lines. Only the doctor chooses agent and dose, and the price is given before you decide.
