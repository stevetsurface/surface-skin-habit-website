HIFU prices in Jakarta 2026: what you pay for, and what you don't

HIFU prices in Jakarta vary widely for three reasons: area, shot count, and who holds the device. Here is how to read them, with Surface's open prices.

HIFU (High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound) delivers heat at 1.5, 3.0 and 4.5 mm to tighten from the support layer, without surgery. At Surface the device is Liftera by Asterasys, Korea, and only a doctor holds it.

HIFU prices in Jakarta range from a few hundred thousand rupiah for salon 'HIFU facials' to over ten million for a full face at premium clinics. Three things make the difference: the area (eyes, double chin, mid-face, neck or full face), shots per area, and whether a doctor performs it.

Surface's prices are open and the same at four clinics: HIFU Ultilift eyes Rp 1,750,000, double chin Rp 3,250,000, mid-face Rp 6,500,000, neck Rp 6,500,000, full face Rp 8,100,000. The Chisel combination (LHALA Peel + HIFU double chin) is Rp 3,350,000 and Jawline Signature (HIFU double chin + meso lipo) Rp 4,150,000.

What you are not paying for: instant results. HIFU works through collagen remodelling, shows fully 8–12 weeks after one session, and is repeated every 6–12 months. If a clinic sells HIFU as six consecutive monthly sessions, ask why.

Who should wait: metal implants in the area, a pacemaker, pregnancy, active infection, and isotretinoin in the last 6 months. The doctor consultation at Surface is free, and the doctor decides the area, not the package.
