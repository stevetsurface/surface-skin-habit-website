Programme

The journey, week by week

Design placeholder

SHOOT: HIFU Liftera on the jawline, device screen visible. 1200×800, 3:2.

  1. Visit 1 · 20 min

    Free doctor consultation + 3-angle photos

    The doctor tells mild laxity (RF), structural laxity (HIFU) and volume loss (meso, boosters) apart. Metal implants, pacemakers and pregnancy decide what is allowed.

  2. Visit 1, same day

    Start: Tighten Start, Tighten & Contour RF

    Peel & Lift. It feels temporarily firmer after the first session; that is the immediate RF effect, not new collagen, and we say so.

  3. Weeks 2–12

    Programme: 3–6 RF sessions, every 2–4 weeks

    Week 4: not much visible in photos yet; this is normal. Week 8: collagen starts to show, the first honest assessment. Contour Signature adds the neck and V-shape contouring.

  4. Week 12 onward

    Doctor review: HIFU or meso if needed

    HIFU is one session per area, full result in 8–12 weeks, repeated every 6–12 months. Jawline Define combines HIFU double chin and meso lipo by the doctor.

FAQ

Questions about this programme

When does it show?

RF: session 3–4. HIFU: 8–12 weeks after one session, as collagen remodels.

Is there downtime?

RF: none. HIFU: mild swelling or tenderness for a few days. Meso: injection points for 1–2 days.

How long does it last?

RF is held with maintenance sessions every few weeks. HIFU lasts 6–12 months before repeating.
