Skin clinics in South Jakarta: how to choose, and where Surface is

Five questions before choosing a skin clinic, and Surface's four locations in Setiabudi, Cilandak, Puri and Cibubur.

2026-10-20

South Jakarta has hundreds of skin clinics, from national chains to doctor boutiques. Five questions separate a medical clinic from a salon: who decides the treatment, a doctor or a salesperson? Are prices published? Are devices named? Is there an interval and a written plan? Does your record work at other branches?

At Surface the answers are: a doctor, with a free consultation; yes, single, 3x and 6x are open; yes, makers and certifications are on the Devices & brands page; yes, every programme has an interval and a week-12 review; yes, one record at four clinics.
