Programme

The journey, week by week

  1. Visit 1 · 20 min

    Free doctor consultation + skin analysis

    The doctor tells pores and roughness (resurfacing), indented scars (subcision or microneedling) and a damaged barrier (soothe first) apart. Isotretinoin and keloid history change the path.

  2. Visit 1, same day

    Start: Texture Start (Diamond Resurface)

    Fine mechanical resurfacing then a LHALA Peel. Reactive skin is routed to the Calm pathway first.

  3. Weeks 4–16

    Programme: Satin, every 4 weeks

    Peel then Nanoporeless microneedling, 3–4 sessions. Redness for 1–3 days. Week 8: pores look finer. The doctor adds PRP or exosomes after needling if needed.

  4. Week 12 onward

    Doctor review: Scar Release if scars are indented

    Indented scars are released by subcision, not sanded down. Then monthly maintenance and sunscreen.

FAQ

Questions about this programme

How many sessions for acne scars?

Microneedling is usually 3–4 sessions, every 4 weeks. Deep indented scars need subcision first. The doctor sets the order after seeing the scars.

Is there downtime?

Diamond Resurface: none. Microneedling: redness for 1–3 days. Subcision: light bruising for a few days.
