Diamond Peel

A diamond-grit tip abrades the layer of dead skin cells, and gentle suction lifts the debris away.

In more depth

The outermost layer of skin is made of dead cells that leave the surface feeling rough and slow product absorption. Abrading it in a controlled way leaves the surface smoother and lets skincare work better.

This is purely mechanical, with no chemicals and no heat, so it suits skin that does not tolerate chemical peels.

What it is used for

  • Dead cell build-up
  • Rough texture
  • Clogged pores
  • Shallow acne marks
  • Skin that looks tired

Clear · Acne, texture & pores

Wait if
