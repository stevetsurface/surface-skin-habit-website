How it works

A radiofrequency current heats the deeper layer of skin evenly until collagen fibres tighten and new collagen production is triggered.

Downtime

Non-invasive RF often allows a quick return to usual activities. Temporary warmth, redness, swelling, or tenderness can still occur. This guide is not for RF microneedling.

OFTEN TEMPORARY

What you may notice

A warm sensation, temporary redness, mild swelling, or tenderness.

Potential risks and side effects

Excess heat can cause burns, blisters, pigment changes, or scarring. Tell the clinician promptly if the heat feels painful.

Before your treatment

Mention implanted electronic devices, metal in the treatment area, pregnancy, skin problems, and medicines so the clinician can assess suitability.

When to contact us

Contact the clinic if discomfort is increasing, redness is spreading, blisters appear, or recovery is different from what was explained. Seek urgent medical care for vision changes, breathing or swallowing difficulty, or severe symptoms.
