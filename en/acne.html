Programme

The journey, week by week

  1. Visit 1 · 20 min

    Free doctor consultation + skin analysis

    The doctor looks at your acne type and screens for what changes the protocol: isotretinoin, pregnancy, keloid history, medication from another doctor.

  2. Visit 1, same day

    Start: Hydraderm + LHALA Peel

    A deep cleanse by the therapist, then a doctor peel (Clear Start). New customers can start at the Hydraderm first-visit price of Rp 799,000.

  3. Weeks 2–12

    Programme: 3 or 6 sessions, every 2–3 weeks

    Active acne moves up to Acne Reset: a doctor SA peel then PDT. Week 4: fewer new breakouts. Week 8: the pattern settles, the point where people most often stop, just when it should continue.

  4. Week 12 onward

    Doctor review, then hold

    The doctor decides: enough, continue, or move to the marks (Scar Release, laser). The Skin Habit Plan at Rp 950,000 a month holds the result with one facial a month and a doctor peel every 3 months.

What we promise

  • The sequence explained before you pay
  • Your next appointment booked before you leave
  • Every peel by a doctor; Acne Shots doctor-only
  • Written aftercare and sunscreen before you leave the clinic

What we don't promise

  • No promise of a 'cure' or 'acne-free'. Acne is a medical condition.
  • Severe nodulocystic acne is not treated in clinic; it is referred to a specialist.
  • No before-and-after photos without written consent.

FAQ

Questions about this programme

How long until I see something?

Inflamed lesions usually settle within 1–2 weeks. The breakout pattern stabilises by session 3–4. Marks need a separate programme once the acne is controlled.

Does it hurt?

The peel feels warm and stings for 1–2 minutes, then is neutralised. PDT feels like nothing.

I'm on acne medication from another doctor.

Tell us at the consultation. Isotretinoin in the last 6 months means peels and laser wait; facials and PDT can go ahead.
