TIME TO PLAN

Downtime

OFTEN TEMPORARY

What you may notice

Warmth, redness, mild swelling, and temporary sensitivity; some pigment treatments cause darkening or crusting.

Potential risks and side effects

Burns or blisters, darker or lighter patches, scarring, and eye injury are possible. Skin tone, recent tanning, medicines, settings, and eye protection all matter.

Before your treatment

Tell the doctor about recent sun exposure and medicines. Use the supplied eye protection and follow the individual sun-care and aftercare plan.

When to contact us

Contact the clinic if discomfort is increasing, redness is spreading, blisters appear, or recovery is different from what was explained. Seek urgent medical care for vision changes, breathing or swallowing difficulty, or severe symptoms.
