ENERGY

Biaxis Q-Switched Nd:YAG

Very short laser pulses break clusters of pigment inside the skin into fine particles, which the body then clears on its own.

Understand the treatment ↗ENERGY

IPL Cellec

Broad-spectrum light is absorbed by pigment and fine vessels, then converted to heat that makes both fade.

Understand the treatment ↗ENERGY

HIFU Liftera

Ultrasound waves are focused to a single point deep in the skin, heating it until the connective tissue contracts and the body lays down new collagen.

Understand the treatment ↗ENERGY

Pollogen RF

A radiofrequency current heats the deeper layer of skin evenly until collagen fibres tighten and new collagen production is triggered.

Understand the treatment ↗ENERGY

Nanopore

Hundreds of very fine micro-punctures trigger the skin's own repair process, filling in scar tissue and smoothing texture.

Understand the treatment ↗ENERGY

PDT Celluma

An LED panel emits light at specific wavelengths that skin cells absorb, calming inflammation and speeding recovery.

Understand the treatment ↗FACIAL

Aquapure H2O

The handpiece sprays solution and vacuums it straight back, lifting what is inside the pores and replacing it with serum.

Understand the treatment ↗FACIAL

Hydraderm

The handpiece abrades the layer of dead cells while keeping the skin wet with serum, so it exfoliates without drying out.

Understand the treatment ↗FACIAL

Oxy Gun

A stream of pressurised oxygen pushes serum into the skin surface with no needles and no abrasion.

Understand the treatment ↗FACIAL

Diamond Peel

A diamond-grit tip abrades the layer of dead skin cells, and gentle suction lifts the debris away.

Understand the treatment ↗DOCTOR

Chemical Peel

An acid solution loosens the bonds between dead skin cells so the top layer sheds and is replaced by newer skin.

Understand the treatment ↗

The information here is general. It is not a diagnosis, nor medical advice for your own skin. Only a doctor who examines you in person can decide what is appropriate. A doctor consultation at Surface is always free.
